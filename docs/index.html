About this data

  • Source: BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, annual averages. These are counts of jobs covered by unemployment insurance, about 95% of U.S. jobs.
  • NAICS vs. SIC: BLS switched industry classification systems in 2001. NAICS-based data is available for 1990 onward; the older SIC-based series covers 1975–2000. The industry codes do not map one-to-one.
  • Suppression: BLS withholds figures that could reveal an individual employer. Establishment counts are still published for those rows.
  • Levels: Domain and supersector are BLS groupings of NAICS sectors, not part of NAICS itself.
